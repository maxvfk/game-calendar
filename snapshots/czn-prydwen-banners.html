FAQ

When Does The Current Banner End?

Each banner card shows a live countdown and the exact end date for your selected server region. Use the Server Region toggle to switch between NA, EU, and Asia times. Global games with only one server only have a single set of countdowns.

What is the current Chaos Zero Nightmare Banner?

The current Chaos Zero Nightmare (CZN) banners are listed in the "Current Banners" section above, with their featured 5-Stars and end dates

Who is the next Chaos Zero Nightmare banner?

The next confirmed banners appear in the "Next Banners" sections with their expected release date. Teased characters that are not yet scheduled are listed separately under "Upcoming Banners". If no characters have been officially teased by the developers these sections may not be present on the banner page temporarily.

What is the difference between “next” and “upcoming”

Next banners have an officially announced or strongly indicated schedule. Upcoming banners have only been revealed and stay marked TBA until an official notice locks the phase and dates.

How Frequently Are Banners Updated

The Prydwen team aims to update banner schedules within 24 hours of their official release but will always wait for official confirmation on information before publication and will not use leaks.
